01 / about

Who I am

I work as a penetration tester at Dawateislami, where I assess web and mobile applications from scoping to re-test.

Most of my findings are the ordinary, damaging kind: cross-site scripting, SQL injection, broken access control (IDOR) and credentials left where they should not be. On Android I use Frida, JADX and ADB to read what an app really does at runtime and how it stores data.

A finding is only useful if someone can act on it, so every report I write has an executive summary, a CVSS score, a proof of concept and a fix the developers can apply. I then re-test with them until it is closed.
